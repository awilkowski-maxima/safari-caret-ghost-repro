Safari: stale caret left behind in a ProseMirror editor

  1. Click into the editor.
  2. Put the caret at the start of a line near the bottom. Not the first line, which never reproduces it.
  3. Press Shift+Return a few times. It has to be Shift+Return, which inserts a hard break inside the paragraph. Plain Return splits the paragraph into a new block and does not reproduce it. Deleting lines can also produce the ghost, when the delete happens to scroll the box, but it is less reliable than inserting.
  4. A second caret stays painted at the caret's previous screen position, frozen at whatever opacity the blink animation was at. Moving the caret over it or typing erases it.
The box is short enough that ProseMirror scrolls the caret into view on each edit, holding it at a fixed screen position.
