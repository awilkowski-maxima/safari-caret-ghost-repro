Safari: stale caret left behind in a ProseMirror editor

  1. Click into the editor.
  2. Put the caret at the start of a line near the bottom.
  3. Press Shift+Enter a few times. Deleting a line from the middle of the content sometimes reproduces it too, less reliably than inserting.
  4. A second caret stays painted at the caret's previous screen position, frozen at whatever opacity the blink animation was at. Moving the caret over it or typing erases it, so don't do either while looking.
The box is short enough that ProseMirror scrolls the caret into view on each edit, holding it at a fixed screen position.
